# Code of Conduct

Dataset Quality Auditor is intended to be a professional, respectful project.

Participants are expected to:

- Be constructive and considerate.
- Welcome different experience levels and perspectives.
- Focus technical discussion on evidence, tradeoffs, and maintainability.
- Avoid harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, or contributions that do not meet these
expectations.
